Rentritz Dubai

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

1. Who We Are

Rentritz Dubai ("Rentritz", "we", "us", "our") operates the Rentritz platform at rentritz.com. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and store it, and what rights you have over your data. By using the platform you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data - your full name, email address, chosen role (tenant or landlord), and encrypted password when you register
  • Consultation data - the category you selected, the answers you gave in the decision tree, and the scenario output delivered to you
  • Payment data - transaction references, amounts, and statuses for credit purchases. We do not store full card numbers or CVV codes; payment card data is handled entirely by Network International (nGenius)
  • Usage data - login timestamps, IP addresses associated with login events, and session activity used for security and fraud prevention

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver consultation outputs matched to your situation
  • To process credit purchases and maintain your credit balance
  • To send transactional emails such as password reset and account notifications
  • To protect the security and integrity of the platform, including detecting and preventing abuse, brute-force attempts, and fraudulent transactions
  • To comply with applicable UAE law and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your consultation data to serve advertising.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under applicable data protection law:

  • Contract performance - processing necessary to deliver the service you have registered for
  • Legitimate interests - security monitoring, fraud prevention, and improving platform reliability
  • Legal obligation - where we are required by UAE law to retain or disclose certain records

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on secure infrastructure. Passwords are stored as salted hashes using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. These include rate limiting, brute-force detection, CSRF protection, and encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We retain your account and consultation data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service providers:

  • Network International (nGenius) - payment processing. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your payment card data
  • Gmail SMTP - transactional email delivery for password resets and account notifications

These providers are engaged under data processing agreements and are only permitted to use your data to provide their services to us.

7. Cookies

Rentritz uses a single session cookie to maintain your login state. This cookie is essential for the platform to function and is set only after you sign in. It is deleted when you sign out or when your session expires.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or similar services.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 9. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

9. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, to exercise your data rights, or to report a data protection concern, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@rentritz.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We will notify you of material changes by email where we hold a current email address for you.

Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
