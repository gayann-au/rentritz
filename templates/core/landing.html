Know Your Rights

Key facts every Dubai tenant and landlord should know about UAE property law.

01
12-Month Eviction Notice
A landlord must give a minimum of 12 months written notice served via notary or registered mail to repossess a property for personal use. Shorter notices are legally invalid.
Dubai Law No. 33/2008, Article 25
02
RERA Caps Rent Increases
Rent increases are strictly regulated by the RERA rental index. If your current rent is within 10% of the index value, your landlord cannot raise it at all during renewal.
RERA Rent Increase Calculator, Decree No. 43/2013
03
Deposit Cannot Exceed 5%
Security deposits for unfurnished apartments are capped at 5% of annual rent, and 10% for furnished units. Landlords must return it within 30 days of lease termination.
Dubai Law No. 26/2007, Article 20
