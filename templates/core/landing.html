II. The Law, Distilled

Three statutes that decide most disputes.

The clauses that quietly govern Dubai tenancy. Cited, distilled, no latin required.

i.
Dubai Law No. 33/2008 . Art. 25(2)

Twelve months. In writing. Through a notary.

A landlord seeking possession for personal use must serve twelve months written notice through a notary public. A shorter notice has no legal effect.

ii.
Decree No. 43/2013 . RERA Index

Within ten percent? The rent does not move.

If your rent sits within ten percent of the RERA index for comparable units, no increase at renewal is permitted. Above that, a strict five-step grid applies.

iii.
Dubai Law No. 26/2007 . Art. 20

Five percent. Returnable.

Security deposits are capped at five percent of annual rent for unfurnished units, ten percent for furnished. Returnable within thirty days, less only documented deductions.
